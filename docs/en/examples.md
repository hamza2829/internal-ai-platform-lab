# Application examples

All examples below are invented; none describes an actual department, customer, or company.

- **Sales:** prepare discovery questions from a fictional prospect summary; never invent a discount or send the follow-up.
- **Product development:** convert a fictional product question into a falsifiable hypothesis, metric, and small experiment.
- **Customer service:** draft a reversible troubleshooting step and escalation path; do not change accounts or promise refunds.
- **Marketing:** review a fictional campaign claim for evidence and accessibility; do not invent testimonials or results.

Each skill lives in `library/skills/<department>/<skill-name>/SKILL.md` and contains instructions plus an example. Scores in `evals/skills.json` are rubric targets, not a measured model benchmark.
