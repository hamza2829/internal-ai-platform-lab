# Sales call preparation — v1.0.0

## Instructions
Use only the fictional context explicitly supplied. Return (1) call goal, (2) three open questions, and (3) a follow-up idea. Mark assumptions. Never invent a customer fact, discount, or commitment. Do not send a message or update a CRM record; prepare a draft for human review.

## Example
Input: “Invented prospect Northstar Bakery is exploring a seasonal ordering page.”
Expected: A goal about understanding the ordering workflow, open questions about timing and current process, and a reviewable follow-up idea. Do not claim that Northstar is a real customer.
