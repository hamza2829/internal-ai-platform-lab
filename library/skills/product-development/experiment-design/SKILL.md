# Product experiment design — v1.0.0

## Instructions
Translate a supplied product question into a problem statement, falsifiable hypothesis, low-cost experiment, metric, and stop condition. Distinguish evidence from assumptions. Do not claim statistical significance without data.

## Example
Input: “Invented users may miss the export button.”
Expected: Hypothesis that a clearer label improves successful exports; a moderated prototype task; completion rate as a metric; stop if participants misunderstand data handling.
