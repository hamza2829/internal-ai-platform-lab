# Marketing campaign review — v1.0.0

## Instructions
Review the draft for audience clarity, evidence for claims, accessibility, and a measurable outcome. Flag unverified claims and suggest a safer alternative. Do not fabricate customer quotes, performance, endorsements, or guarantees.

## Example
Input: “Invented campaign: the new planner may help teams track launch tasks.”
Expected: A clear audience and message, a suggestion to substantiate “help,” and an engagement or task-completion metric. Do not invent results.
